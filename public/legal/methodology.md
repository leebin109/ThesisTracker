# ThesisTrack Methodology

Last updated: 2026-05-15

ThesisTrack organizes investment research around written reasoning, source provenance, and decision review.

## 1. Thesis

Users are encouraged to write the key question, thesis, catalysts, risks, and conditions that would change their view before acting.

## 2. Provenance

Metrics should show where they came from when possible. Public filing data, verified APIs, derived metrics, and user-entered assumptions have different confidence levels.

## 3. Replay

Journal and review features help users compare their original reasoning with later outcomes. This is intended for reflection, not for guaranteeing future performance.

## 4. Scores and Coverage

Scores combine available metrics when enough verified data exists. If coverage is low, ThesisTrack should show a limited metrics state rather than presenting a complete score.

## 5. Commercial-Safe Method

Commercial-Safe mode favors official filings and verified endpoints. Sources with unclear or restricted terms are blocked before network requests are made.
